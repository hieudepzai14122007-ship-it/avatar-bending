# Elemental Arts: a spell expansion for Noita

A mod for [Noita](https://noitagame.com/) that adds twenty-eight new elemental spells to the spell pool. They turn up in wands, shops and Holy Mountains like any vanilla spell, and one copy of each is dropped next to you at the start of a new run so you can try them straight away.

## The spells

| Spell | Type | Mana | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Whirlwind** | Projectile | 45 | A slow vortex that bounces around the cave for about 3 seconds. It drags enemies into its core and lifts them off the ground, cuts whatever reaches the core (3.5 slice damage per hit), knocks enemy projectiles off course, flings loose objects, fans flames and blows away gases such as smoke, steam and toxic fumes. |
| **Tidal Orb** | Projectile | 30 | A lobbed sphere of water. It deals 10 damage on a direct hit, then bursts for 15 more with heavy knockback and a fountain of real water. Soaks enemies and puts out fires. The burst does not dig into terrain. |
| **Stone Lance** | Projectile | 50 | A spear of rock that flies through creatures and terrain for a short distance and leaves a solid stone beam along its path. Use it to build bridges, walls and stairs, to plug a hole, or to skewer a row of enemies (15 damage per hit). |
| **Flame Serpent** | Projectile | 35 | A slithering tongue of fire that homes in on the nearest enemy. It deals 13.75 fire damage, sets the target alight, flares up on impact and leaves a trail of real fire behind it. |
| **Glacial Shards** | Projectile | 30 | A fan of three fast icicles. Each deals 7.5 ice damage, freezes the enemy it hits for a moment, and turns any water it touches to ice. |
| **Updraft** | Utility | 35 | A column of rising air that launches you straight up for a quarter of a second and cancels any fall. Good for reaching ledges and for getting out of trouble. It also lifts an enemy that casts it. |
| **Magma Bomb** | Projectile | 70 | A lobbed chunk of molten rock, 12 uses. It deals 20 damage on a direct hit, explodes for 30 more, breaks soft terrain and spills a small pool of real lava. The lava hurts you as much as anything else. |
| **Thunderstrike** | Projectile | 80 | A fast spark that marks a spot. Where it hits, a bolt of the game's own lightning falls from the ceiling above, or from 140 pixels up in the open. The lightning hurts you too if you stand next to the target, and it electrifies water. |
| **Frost Nova** | Static projectile | 60 | A burst of cold around the point of casting, 15 uses. Enemies within 72 pixels are frozen for 2 seconds and take about 15 ice damage. Water turns to ice and lava hardens to rock. You are not frozen. |
| **Fire Pillar** | Projectile | 40 | A lobbed ember that erupts into a column of real fire about 80 pixels tall for nearly 2 seconds. Enemies in the column take about 18 fire damage per second and catch fire. Good for blocking a corridor. |
| **Gale Barrier** | Utility | 60 | A ring of wind that follows you for 4 seconds, 12 uses. Enemy projectiles that fly into it are bounced back out, and enemies that come close are pushed away. It does not stop explosions, melee hits or hazards such as fire and acid. |
| **Quagmire** | Modifier | 8 | The projectile leaves a trail of mud and bogs down what it hits: the target moves much slower for 10 seconds. Adds 2.5 damage. |
| **Waystone** | Utility | 50 | Cast once to leave a glowing waystone where you stand. Cast again to return to it instantly, which uses the stone up. A waystone fades after two minutes. If the spot has since been filled in, for example by your own Stone Lance, you arrive inside the rock. |
| **Avalanche** | Projectile | 25 | A snowball that bounces along and grows as it goes. It starts at 5 damage and gains 1.25 every 4 frames, up to +50 and three times the size after about 2.7 seconds. Briefly freezes what it hits. Weak up close, strong at the end of a long tunnel. |
| **Crescent Gale** | Projectile | 30 | A blade of wind that cuts through enemies (12.5 slice damage per hit), flies about 130 pixels, then curves around and returns to you, cutting them again on the way back. It bounces off walls. |
| **Bubble Prison** | Projectile | 30 | A drifting bubble. The first enemy it touches is sealed inside and carried upwards for 3 seconds, feet off the ground, then dropped. It also soaks the enemy. Barely any damage; it is for taking a dangerous enemy out of the fight. |
| **Gathering Storm** | Projectile | 35 | A bolt that charges while the spell rests. It always deals 7.5 electric damage, plus up to 45 more depending on how long ago you last cast it: nothing in the first third of a second, the full bonus after 4 seconds. Rapid fire wastes it; a patient shot hits very hard. |
| **Ember Halo** | Utility | 55 | Three embers circle you for 7 seconds, about 24 pixels out. Each deals 6 fire damage to an enemy it passes through and sets it alight. The embers pass through walls and do not burn you. |
| **Riptide** | Projectile | 25 | A bolt of water that deals 7.5 damage, soaks its target, then drags it towards you for 0.8 seconds. Use it to pull an enemy off a ledge, out of cover, or into a trap you have laid. |
| **Earthshaker** | Utility | 60 | Hurls you straight down. The moment you land, a shockwave deals 40 damage to everything within 34 pixels and throws loose objects about. It does not hurt you and does not break the ground. Cast while standing, it is a plain stomp. |
| **Frost Sentinel** | Static projectile | 90 | An ice crystal that drifts a short way from the wand and hangs there for 8 seconds, 12 uses. About three times a second it fires an icicle (7.5 ice damage, brief freeze) at the nearest enemy within 170 pixels that it has a clear line to. |
| **Shatter** | Modifier | 10 | The projectile always lands a critical hit on frozen enemies and gains 2.5 ice damage. Pairs with Frost Nova, Glacial Shards, Avalanche and Frost Sentinel. |
| **Forked Lightning** | Modifier | 25 | When the projectile ends, by hitting something or by running out of time, up to two bolts arc from that spot to the nearest enemies within 110 pixels that are in clear view. Each bolt deals 7.5 electric damage. The enemy the projectile was touching is skipped, since it has already been hit. |
| **Stone Form** | Utility | 80 | You turn to living stone for about 1.7 seconds: nothing can hurt you, and you cannot move. 6 uses, and it cannot be made unlimited. For surviving an explosion you cannot outrun. |
| **Tidal Wave** | Projectile | 45 | A rolling wave that travels about 270 pixels, passing through enemies and sweeping them along with it. It deals 5 damage per hit and soaks them, then breaks for 10 more with a splash of real water. Use it to clear a corridor or push a crowd off a ledge. |
| **Smoke Veil** | Utility | 50 | You vanish in a puff of real smoke and are invisible for 6 seconds, 10 uses. It uses the game's own invisibility, so the game's usual rules for when it breaks apply. |
| **Elemental Volley** | Projectile | 110 | Five projectiles at once in a fan: a Flame Serpent, a Riptide bolt, a Glacial Shard, a storm bolt and a Crescent Gale. Each behaves as it does in its own spell; the storm bolt is uncharged. |
| **Elemental Attunement** | Modifier | 12 | Each cast infuses the projectile with the next element in a fixed cycle: **air** (60% faster, more accurate, strong knockback), **water** (soaks the target, always crits soaked targets, leaves a water trail), **earth** (+10 damage, +15% crit chance, 15% slower), **fire** (+6 fire damage, ignites the target, leaves a fire trail). |

For reference, the starting Spark Bolt deals 3 damage.

Stone Lance and Flame Serpent start their stone and fire a short distance from the wand, so they do not form on top of you. Fire and lava still burn you if you walk into them.

## Install

1. Download [`release/ElementalArts-1.5.0-Noita.zip`](../release/ElementalArts-1.5.0-Noita.zip) and unzip it.
2. Copy the `elemental_arts` folder into the `mods` folder of your Noita install. On Steam: right-click Noita → **Manage → Browse local files**, then open `mods`.
   The folder must keep the name `elemental_arts`, because the mod loads its files by that path.
3. Start Noita, open **Mods**, and enable **Elemental Arts - Spell Expansion**.
4. Start a **new game**. The mod needs no unsafe mode.

## Settings

In **Mods → Mod settings**:

- **Start with the new spells** (on by default): drops one copy of each new spell next to you at the start of a new run. Turn it off to find the spells only through normal play. The change applies from the next new game.

## Compatibility

- Made for the current release version of Noita (1.0 and later).
- Adds spells only. It does not change any vanilla spell, enemy or material, so it should sit alongside other spell mods.
- Spell IDs all start with `ELEMENTAL_ARTS_`.

## Status

The mod has not been run inside Noita yet. It was built against Noita's own data files, and these checks pass: every XML component and attribute it uses also appears in the vanilla files; every vanilla file, material and sound it refers to exists; and all of the mod's Lua (the spell definitions, the nineteen spell scripts, the settings and the start-up code) runs under LuaJIT against a stand-in for the game's functions. Balance numbers are a first pass and may need tuning after play.

## Files

```
elemental_arts/
  mod.xml                       mod name and description
  init.lua                      registers the spells, drops the starter spells
  settings.lua                  the mod setting
  files/
    actions.lua                 the twenty-eight spell definitions
    entities/projectiles/       the projectiles, plus what they leave behind (splashes, the fire column)
    entities/misc/              the waystone, and the effects attached to enemies, projectiles and the caster
    scripts/                    the scripts behind the spells that need one (nineteen in all)
    ui_gfx/                     spell icons
    projectiles_gfx/            projectile sprites
```

The icons and sprites are generated by [`tools/gen_noita_gfx.py`](../tools/gen_noita_gfx.py).
